Drop your installation videos here (.mp4, .webm, .mov).

The gallery page (`/gallery`) automatically picks up every video in this
folder - no code changes needed.

Important: self-hosting video files works fine for local development, but
on real hosting (Hostinger) large videos are slow to load and burn through
bandwidth quotas fast. For anything more than a couple of short clips,
upload to YouTube (as "Unlisted" if you don't want it publicly searchable)
and embed those instead - ask to have this switched over once you have
your YouTube links.
